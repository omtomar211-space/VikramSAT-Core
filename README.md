# Mission VikramSAT 🛰️🇮🇳

> **"Forget coding assignments. We are coding destiny."**

**Mission VikramSAT** is a student-led, nation-wide space initiative under **Team Antariksh**, bringing together passionate engineering students across India to design, build, and deploy a professional-grade student satellite targeting Low Earth Orbit (LEO).

---

## 🚀 About the Mission
VikramSAT serves as an inclusive platform for undergraduate innovators to bridge the gap between theoretical academia and hands-on aerospace engineering. By focusing on **Software-Defined Satellite (SDS)** architecture, modern embedded systems, and robust telemetry, our multi-college consortium is building real space hardware from the ground up.

---

## 🛠️ Core Engineering Subsystems
Our development framework is divided into three primary technical departments:

1. **Software & Telemetry**
   * Flight software development and on-board computer (OBC) logic.
   * Real-time telemetry data processing, packet framing, and radio communication protocols.
2. **Hardware & Circuits**
   * Power distribution, sensor integration, and circuit board design.
   * Microcontroller (ESP32/Raspberry Pi) interfacing and power management systems.
3. **Structure & Systems**
   * Mechanical chassis and structural framework design (CAD modeling).
   * Thermal management, vibration tolerance, and component integration.
